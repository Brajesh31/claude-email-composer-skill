# Email Composer

Use this skill to draft, revise, or evaluate emails for professional, personal, academic, and customer-support contexts.

## Workflow
1. Identify recipient, relationship, purpose, desired outcome, and any deadline.
2. Extract only facts supplied by the user or present in source material.
3. Choose tone and level of formality appropriate to the relationship.
4. Put the main point early and make the requested action explicit.
5. Use a concise subject line and readable paragraphs.
6. Check names, dates, amounts, attachments, links, and commitments.
7. Offer a shorter or warmer alternative only when it would materially help.

## Rules
- Never invent names, titles, email addresses, facts, attachments, or prior conversations.
- Do not imply an attachment is included unless the user confirms it.
- Avoid manipulative urgency, false claims, and unnecessary flattery.
- Preserve the user's intent while improving clarity and tone.
- Do not send an email unless an authorized email integration is connected and the user explicitly approves sending.

## Output
Provide subject, complete body, and optional notes about facts the user must verify. Use `templates/email-brief.md` and `references/tone-guide.md`.
