# Follow-up Email Example

**Subject:** Following up on [TOPIC]

Hello [NAME],

I wanted to follow up on my message from [DATE] about [TOPIC]. Could you let me know whether there is an update or if you need any additional information from me?

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,  
[YOUR NAME]

---

Replace every bracketed field before sending. Do not imply that a prior message was sent unless that is accurate.
